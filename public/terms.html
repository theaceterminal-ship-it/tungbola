2. What Tungbola Is

Tungbola is a digital caller: it lets you upload tambola ticket sheets, call out numbers, and automatically track which prizes have been won. It exists because a lot of tambola games still get played with printed paper sheets and a manual caller — and all that printing adds up to real, avoidable paper waste. Reducing that is the whole reason this app exists. We built a tool to replace the paper, not a gambling product.

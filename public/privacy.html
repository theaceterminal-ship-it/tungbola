Privacy Policy

Tungbola — what we collect, and why

In short: We collect your name and phone number to keep a record of your purchases. That's it — we don't process it for anything else, and we don't sell or share it.

1. What We Collect

When you register or activate a subscription, we collect:

  • Your name — so we know who a purchase or subscription belongs to.
  • Your phone number — so we can identify your account and, if you've given it to us, send you a reminder when it's time to renew.

2. Why We Collect It

Purely to keep a record of your purchases and orders — which plan you bought, when, and the status of your subscription or wallet balance. We don't use your name or number for anything beyond that.

3. What We Don't Do

  • We do not process your name or phone number for any purpose beyond tracking your own purchase record.
  • We do not analyse, profile, or build marketing data from it.
  • We do not sell it.
  • We do not share it with any third party, except where the law requires it of us.

4. Device Identifier

To enforce the device limit on a subscription, the app stores a random identifier on your device (not your name or number — just a random code). It's only used to count how many devices are using a given key, and it never leaves your device except to check that count.

5. Ticket Sheets You Upload

Ticket sheets you load into the app to play are processed on your own device, in your browser — reading the numbers off the file so the app can call them. They are not uploaded to or stored on our servers as part of this process.

6. How Long We Keep It

We keep your name and phone number for as long as your account or subscription is active, so your purchase history stays accurate. If you'd like it removed, contact us (see below) and we'll delete it.

7. Your Choices

You can ask us to tell you what we have on file, correct it, or delete it at any time — just get in touch using the contact details in the app or in our Terms & Conditions.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
